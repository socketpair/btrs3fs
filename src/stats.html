Zones

One cell per zone in disk order, left to right; partly written zones fill from the bottom. Hover a cell for its details; the switch above dims all zones but data or metadata ones. “Upload buffered data” stores everything held in RAM now but does not commit btrfs — use “Commit the filesystem” on the btrfs tab for that. “Concatenate fragmented zones” merges the S3 objects of every zone that has more than one.

Operations

Removing empty block groups is btrfs balance start -dusage=0: their zones are reset and their S3 objects deleted. Committing is syncfs(): btrfs writes its transaction, so every written file is stored in S3.
